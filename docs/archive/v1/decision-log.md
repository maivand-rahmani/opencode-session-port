## Notation

- **DECIDED** — owner-approved V1 scope/direction; does not silently finalize unlisted technical details.
- **PROPOSED** — recommended direction; not agreed.
- **OPEN** — unresolved; do not infer an answer. Resolve before the dependent behavior is finalized/released; independent work may proceed.
- **(CANDIDATE)** — research-only reference; not selected, not validated.
- **SUPERSEDED** — retained for provenance; replaced by a later entry; not a current direction.
- **RESOLVED** — an OPEN decision the owner has answered; retained for cross-reference; the answer is stated in the entry's notes.
- **PARTIALLY RESOLVED** — an OPEN decision the owner has answered in part; the answered subdecisions are stated in the entry's notes and the unresolved remainder stays OPEN.
